# Authorized use only

soul-toolkit is built for **learning and authorized security testing**: your own
machines, lab platforms such as TryHackMe and Hack The Box, CTF events, and
engagements where you hold written permission from the system owner.

Scanning, brute-forcing or probing systems you do not have permission to test is
illegal in most countries and can cause real harm. You are solely responsible
for how you use this software. The author accepts no liability for misuse or
for any damage caused by it.

If you are unsure whether you are allowed to scan something, you are not.
